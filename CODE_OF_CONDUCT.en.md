# Code of conduct

<p><a href="CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md">Italiano</a> | <a href="CODE_OF_CONDUCT.en.md"><strong>English</strong></a></p>

Everyone taking part in Pocket Travel pledges to make the project an open, welcoming and harassment-free space for everyone, regardless of age, body, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, sexual orientation, experience, education, economic status, nationality, appearance, origin and religion.

## Expected behavior

- Empathy and kindness toward other people.
- Respect for differing opinions and experiences.
- Constructive feedback about the work, never the person, given and accepted gracefully.
- Responsibility for one's mistakes, with an apology to those affected.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Sexualized language, imagery or attention.
- Insults, trolling and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Any other conduct inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

The code applies in all project spaces (repository, issues, pull requests, wiki) and when representing the project in public.

## Consequences

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject contributions that do not follow the code, and decides the consequences by severity:

| Level | When | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Correction | inappropriate language or behavior | private warning with an explanation; a public apology may be requested |
| Warning | a single or repeated violation | warning and no interaction with the people involved for a set period |
| Temporary ban | a serious or sustained violation | ban from any interaction with the community for a set period |
| Permanent ban | repeated violations, sustained harassment, aggression toward groups of people | permanent ban from the community |
